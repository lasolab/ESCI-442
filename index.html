ESCI 442/542 — Introduction to Remote Sensing

Lab materials for an undergraduate and graduate course on the physics of remote sensing, raster analysis and cloud computing in Google Earth Engine. Each lab opens in your browser; they build on each other, so work through them in order.

  1. 01The Local Baseline
  2. 02The Cloud Shift
  3. 03Matching Sensor to Purpose
  4. 04Building and Reducing Image Collections
  5. 05The 2021 Heat Dome in the Salish Sea
  6. 06Classifying the Salish Sea
  7. 07Accurate Against What?
  8. 08Change Detection and Time Series
